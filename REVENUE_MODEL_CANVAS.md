# VOWVY Revenue Model Canvas

Framework area 14. Rule applied: Revenue Imagination Before Revenue Judgment - one honest line per model, then shortlist, then owner decision.

## Step 1 - Brainstorm (full menu)

- subscription SaaS: YES - the natural personal plan; owner hypothesis 5-8 dollars/month over 10 free containers.
- freemium: YES - already the owner plan; free tier proves value before asking for money.
- usage-based pricing: Partial - could meter AI captures for heavy business users; too fiddly for consumers.
- transaction fees: LATER - a cut of marketplace sales if direct publishing lands; depends on platform rules.
- data licensing: NO for personal data, ever. Aggregate anonymous trends maybe someday; reputational risk outweighs it now.
- API access: LATER - insurers or movers pulling inventories with consent.
- reports and intelligence products: YES SOON - insurance documentation reports (a polished PDF home inventory) is a natural paid export; idea already in the audit brief.
- lead generation: Partial - referring users to movers or storage could earn fees; keep clearly labeled.
- marketplace fees: LATER - same as transaction fees.
- white-label licensing: YES - owner already planned (box company under own brand; setup fee + monthly).
- enterprise licensing: LATER - storage chains, large movers.
- consulting: NO - does not scale, distracts.
- managed services: Partial - estate cleanout done-for-you capture crews could be a partner play, not a VOWVY staff play.
- affiliate and referral revenue: Partial - boxes, labels, moving supplies links; small money, fine.
- sponsorships: NO meaningful fit.
- grants: Unlikely fit; nonprofit inventory use exists but is not core.
- donor-funded tools: NO.
- training and certification: NO at this scale.
- templates and downloadables: Partial - printable QR label sheets as a cheap paid pack or free growth hook (probably the latter).
- premium support: Bundle into business tier, not standalone.
- hardware and QR kits: YES - pre-printed branded QR label kits, physical and giftable; pairs with the box company concept.
- strategic partnerships: YES - insurers (free benefit to policyholders, owner-identified), box rental, movers.
- acquisition / build-to-sell: KEEP OPEN - plausible acquirers: moving platforms, storage operators, insurance tech. See EXIT_READINESS.md.

## Step 2 - Shortlist (best fit of customer, value, effort)

1. Freemium personal subscription (5-8 dollars/month) - core engine, owner-priced.
2. B2B plans + white label for movers/box/storage companies (50-200 dollars/month) - fastest real money, one pilot already identified.
3. Marketplace/resale path - quality-gated listing exports with AI titles, descriptions, categories and price-range support; later direct publishing (eBay and Etsy have APIs, Facebook is restrictive) and possibly per-listing or pro-tier pricing. The Junk Drawer feeds it; the Image Quality Gate and Marketplace Quality Gate protect it; subtle Created with VOWVY branding makes every listing an ad.

Supporting: insurance documentation report as a paid export; QR label kits as product and growth loop.

## Step 3 - Decision (OWNER)

Owner-stated priority stands: keep free for now, validate with real users, build the paywall around what users ask to pay for; B2B partnerships are the fastest path to revenue. Primary: freemium subscription. Secondary: B2B/white-label. Marketplace path: build toward it behind quality gates. To be recorded in DECISIONS.md when the owner confirms.

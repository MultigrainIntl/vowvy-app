# VOWVY Business Plan

Framework area 13, using the Agent Team OS business model canvas. Plain English.

1. CUSTOMER: Primary - people moving house or managing storage who fear the mystery box. Secondary (B2B) - moving and box rental companies, storage facilities, professional organizers, estate services, resellers.

2. PROBLEM: You cannot remember what is in your boxes, and finding anything means opening everything. For businesses: customer calls, lost items, no differentiation.

3. VALUE: Photograph and forget - AI makes belongings searchable years later. For B2B: a premium service add-on and fewer support calls. For resellers: inventory becomes listable income (marketplace path).

4. ALTERNATIVES: Doing nothing (most common), spreadsheets, marker-on-box, generic inventory apps that demand careful data entry (which is exactly the friction VOWVY removes).

5. CHANNELS: SEO at the moment of pain, B2B partnerships with QR-printed boxes, the marketplace branding loop, printed QR labels as physical ads. See GTM_PLAN.md.

6. REVENUE: Freemium personal plan, business plans, white label, insurance partnerships, and the future marketplace path. Owner has already priced first hypotheses (5-8 dollars per month personal, 50-200 per month business). Full menu and shortlist in REVENUE_MODEL_CANVAS.md.

7. COSTS: Firebase (storage, bandwidth, functions), Gemini per photo, domain and email tooling, and owner time. Currently unmeasured - see RESOURCE_BUDGET.md. Client-side photo compression already keeps storage costs sane.

8. MOAT: Honest answer - thin today. Candidate moats: the captured inventory itself (switching cost grows with every photo), the B2B QR workflow integration, and brand trust around a sensitive data category. The AI itself is not a moat; everyone has AI.

9. RISKS (the honest three):
   1. Key-person and code risk: the real codebase is not in GitHub; one laptop failure is an existential event until synced.
   2. Habit risk: if users do not keep capturing, the product quietly dies; activation and habit metrics (ANALYTICS_PLAN.md) are the early-warning system.
   3. Platform risk on the marketplace path: Facebook Marketplace has no friendly public API; build export-first, API-second.

## One-minute summary

VOWVY makes physical belongings searchable with photos and AI. Individuals pay a small subscription for unlimited memory of their stuff; businesses pay real money to offer it as a service layer on boxes and storage; resellers turn the same captures into marketplace listings. The wedge is the moment of moving - the one day everyone wishes they had this.

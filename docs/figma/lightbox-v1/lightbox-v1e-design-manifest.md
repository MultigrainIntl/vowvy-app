## Locked Design Rules

These rules are locked and may not be changed without explicit GAJ approval.

1. **Photo visibility:** The selected photo/image must render in the large photo stage.
2. **AI content:** AI description and AI tags remain visible on the main lightbox screen when available.
3. **Add Photo:** The Add Photo/camera control remains visible on the main lightbox screen.
4. **Sell:** Add to Sell / Added remains visible on the main lightbox screen.
5. **Add Photo in More:** Add Photo must NOT appear in the More sheet.
6. **Sell in More:** Add to Sell / Added must NOT appear in the More sheet.
7. **More sheet contents — exactly:** Delete (red), Move photo, Move Box, Redo AI, Privacy. No other actions.
8. **Delete color:** Delete is styled red.
9. **Privacy placement:** The Privacy action belongs in More.
10. **Privacy compact status:** Compact Privacy / Follow parent status may remain visible on the main surface as shown in Figma.
11. **No unauthorized changes:** Do not hide, move, remove, rename, restyle, or reclassify any Figma-approved visible element without explicit GAJ approval.
12. **Source of truth:** Do not use failed preview screenshots as the source of truth. The Figma frames in this directory are the authoritative design reference.

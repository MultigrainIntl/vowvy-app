## The moving company contributor workflow as a sales wedge

The pitch to movers: your crew photographs and QR-labels every box during packing; the customer gets a searchable inventory of their move as a premium add-on; you get fewer where-is-my-X calls and a differentiator. Requirements: temporary contributor access that auto-expires at job end, per-crew attribution, and owner revoke. The collaborator foundation already deployed makes this credible to build. Start with ONE local pilot (the box company contact already identified) before building general-purpose features.

## Launch sequence (smallest credible first)

1. Fix DNS + OG image (credibility), add analytics (measurement)
2. Personal-use traction push: one SEO page per persona, share loop via QR labels
3. One B2B pilot with a box rental or moving company
4. Marketplace export beta (resellers are the most viral persona - their listings are public)

Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2026

Vowvy is designed to help people organize and track their personal belongings. This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") describes what you may and may not do on Vowvy. It applies to all content you upload — including photos, item names, and notes — and to all actions you take within the service.

This policy exists to protect users, to comply with applicable law, and to ensure Vowvy remains a safe and trustworthy service. By using Vowvy, you agree to this AUP.

Prohibited Content

The following types of content are strictly prohibited on Vowvy. You may not upload, store, share, or transmit content that:

  • Depicts or facilitates child exploitation. This includes any sexual content involving minors (child sexual abuse material / CSAM), content that sexualizes children or teenagers in any way, or any material designed to groom, exploit, or abuse minors. This is reported to NCMEC and law enforcement without exception.
  • Is pornographic, lewd, or sexually explicit. This includes nude or semi-nude images intended to be sexual, fetish content, or any adult sexual content of any kind.
  • Depicts or promotes violence or harm to people. This includes graphic violence, threats of violence, content that promotes self-harm, suicide, or the harming of others.
  • Depicts or promotes cruelty to animals. This includes content showing harm, abuse, or killing of animals outside of lawful hunting or humane slaughter practices.
  • Involves illegal goods or activities. This includes stolen goods, counterfeit items, controlled substances, unlawful weapons, explosives, hazardous materials, or anything whose possession, sale, or distribution is illegal in your jurisdiction.
  • Facilitates harassment, stalking, doxxing, or surveillance. This includes content used to intimidate, harass, or track individuals without their consent, or to expose another person's private information.
  • Facilitates fraud or deception. This includes false ownership claims, scams, phishing material, money laundering, counterfeit goods, or any content intended to deceive or defraud others.
  • Violates intellectual property rights. This includes uploading copyrighted material you do not have the right to use or reproduce.
  • Contains or facilitates malware or unauthorized access. This includes content designed to compromise security systems, bypass authentication, conduct phishing, facilitate unauthorized data collection or scraping, or introduce malware or exploits of any kind.

Prohibited Uses

In addition to prohibited content, the following uses of Vowvy are not permitted:

  • Using Vowvy to conduct or facilitate any business or activity that is unlawful in your jurisdiction
  • Uploading content that you do not have the rights to share
  • Attempting to access, modify, or interfere with another user's data or account
  • Creating multiple accounts to evade a suspension or ban
  • Sharing login credentials with others or allowing unauthorized access to your account
  • Using automated bots, scrapers, or scripts to access or interact with Vowvy without authorization
  • Attempting to circumvent any content moderation, safety feature, or security control

Enforcement

We take violations of this policy seriously. If we identify content that violates this AUP, we may:

  • Remove the content from the platform
  • Suspend or permanently terminate the account responsible
  • Report the content and associated account information to law enforcement or regulatory authorities
  • Report CSAM to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) as required by US federal law

We reserve the right to act on violations at our sole discretion and without advance notice.

Reporting a Violation

If you become aware of content on Vowvy that you believe violates this policy, please report it to us immediately at legal@vowvy.com. Please include as much detail as possible, including any relevant account or content identifiers.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Acceptable Use Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date and may require you to review and re-accept the updated policy before continuing to use Vowvy.

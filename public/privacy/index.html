Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2026

Vowvy ("we," "us," or "our") is a personal inventory and organization tool. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use Vowvy, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By creating an account or using Vowvy, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your email address. Your password is managed by Firebase Authentication (Google) and is never visible to us in plaintext.

Content you store. Vowvy stores the photos, item names, location names, container notes, and other content you choose to upload or enter. This content is stored in your account and is associated with your user ID.

Usage data. We collect information about how you interact with Vowvy — for example, which features you use and how often. This helps us understand what is working and what to improve.

Device and technical data. We may collect your browser type, device type, operating system version, and error logs when problems occur. This data is used only to maintain and improve the service.

Policy acceptance records. When you agree to Vowvy's policies, we store the version of the policies you accepted and the date of acceptance.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Vowvy service
  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure
  • To review content for safety and compliance with our Acceptable Use Policy
  • To send you essential service-related communications (such as account security alerts)
  • To diagnose and fix technical issues

We do not use your content to train AI models or sell your data to advertisers.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data.

Service providers. We use third-party services to operate Vowvy, including Firebase (Google Cloud) for authentication, database, and file storage. These providers process your data only as directed by us and under contractual data protection obligations.

Law enforcement. We may disclose information when required by applicable law, court order, or to protect the safety of users or the public — including when we become aware of content that constitutes illegal activity such as child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

Shared containers. When you share a container with a collaborator, that person can view the photos and items in that container. You control all sharing through the Manage screen. We do not share your content with other users beyond what you explicitly authorize.

4. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Deleted photos and items are retained in a soft-deleted state for a short grace period before permanent deletion. You can delete your account at any time from the Profile settings, which will permanently delete your stored content.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You can delete your account and all associated data directly from the Profile screen within the app. You may contact us at legal@vowvy.com to request a copy of your data or to raise any privacy concerns.

6. Children's Privacy

Vowvy is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Security

We use industry-standard security practices, including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS) and Firebase's authentication and storage security. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of your data.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and may require you to review and re-accept the updated policy before continuing to use Vowvy.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at legal@vowvy.com.
